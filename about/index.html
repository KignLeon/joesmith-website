The "Swiss Army Knife"

Servant. Leader. Educator. Father.

Donate Now

Joe Smith is a graduate of Hampton University with a degree in sociology, bringing a unique lens to understanding people, cultures, and the demographics that impact a community. With more than 15 years of service in K through 12 education, Joe has earned a reputation as a "Swiss Army Knife", a versatile public servant, educator, and advocate who strengthens every community he touches.

Now residing in Lemon Grove, Joe Smith is deeply committed to servant leadership and community driven solutions that uplift residents and strengthen neighborhoods. Guided by faith, integrity, and a commitment to excellence, Joe is a father on a mission, showing up with passion, purpose, and a "whatever it takes" mindset.

His leadership is grounded in service, empathy, and the belief that students and families deserve unwavering advocacy and access to opportunity.
